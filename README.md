### 🏆 Featured project

**CivicPulse: Report. Predict. Verify.**
*Top 100 of 3,500 engineers at Google Cloud Patchamomma 2026*

A Flutter app backed by a five-agent AI pipeline on Google Cloud. Citizens photograph a civic issue; the agents classify it with Gemini Vision, merge duplicates within 200 m, route it to the right department, verify the fix with before/after image comparison, and forecast high-risk wards with BigQuery ML.

`Flutter` `Gemini 1.5 Flash` `Google ADK` `MCP Toolbox` `Cloud Run` `FastAPI` `Firestore` `Pub/Sub` `BigQuery ML` `Looker Studio`
